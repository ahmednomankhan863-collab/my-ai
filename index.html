💰 Making a 30-Second Video (Strategy & Cost)
• Standard AI video models generate 4 to 5 seconds per clip.
• A 30-second video requires 6 consecutive 5-second scenes.
• Free Method: Generate 6 individual scene clips on Kling/Luma, then merge them in CapCut or Clipchamp with free background audio.
